Organic AI

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

Organic AI is an iPhone app that scores food products from their ingredients. This policy explains what the app does with your information. In short: we do not run a server, we do not collect your data, and we do not track you.

What stays on your iPhone

Your answers from onboarding (goal, watch list, diets, allergies, daily goal, target score, reminder time), your scans, saved swaps and progress are stored on your device. If iCloud is turned on for the app, this data is also kept in your own private iCloud database so it can sync between your devices. Apple stores that data; we cannot see it.

Photos and the camera

The camera is used to read barcodes and ingredient labels. Label photos are read on your iPhone with Apple's text recognition and are never uploaded. A small thumbnail of a scanned label is saved with that scan, on your device and in your private iCloud.

Product lookups

When you scan a barcode or search for a product by name, the app sends only the barcode number or the search words to Open Food Facts (openfoodfacts.org), a free and open product database, to fetch the product's ingredients and nutrition data. No account details or personal information are sent. Open Food Facts has its own privacy policy.

Sign in with Apple

Signing in is optional. If you sign in, Apple gives the app an anonymous user identifier and, the first time only, your name and an email address (which can be Apple's private relay address). These are stored in your profile on your device and in your private iCloud. We do not receive them on any server.

Purchases

Subscriptions are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details. The app only checks with Apple whether you have an active subscription.

Notifications

If you turn on reminders, the app schedules them on your device. No notification data leaves your iPhone.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect analytics or usage data.
  • We do not use advertising or tracking, and we do not sell or share data.
  • We do not run our own servers that store your information.

Deleting your data

In the app, go to You → Delete all my data. This removes your scans, saved swaps and settings from your device and, with iCloud on, from your other devices. You can also remove the app's iCloud data in the Settings app under your name → iCloud → Manage Account Storage. To stop using Sign in with Apple for Organic AI, go to Settings → your name → Sign-In & Security → Sign in with Apple.

Children

Organic AI is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Not medical advice

Scores and flags are estimates to help compare products. They are not medical, dietary or allergy advice.
