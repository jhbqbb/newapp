Questions

Good to know.

Where does the product data come from?

Barcode lookups and product search use Open Food Facts, an open database maintained by volunteers. Photos of a label are read on your iPhone and checked against the ingredient database built into the app.

Is my data private?

Your scans and plan stay on your iPhone and, if you use iCloud, in your own private iCloud. A barcode lookup sends only the barcode number. Label photos never leave your device.

Is this medical advice?

No. Scores and flags are estimates to help you compare products. If you have an allergy or a medical condition, always read the package yourself.

What if a product is not in the database?

Take a photo of the ingredient list or type it in. The app scores it from the ingredients alone.
